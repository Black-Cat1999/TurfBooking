Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own equipment? +

We provide basic equipment like bibs and balls for football, and stumps for cricket. However, you are welcome to bring your own personal gear like bats or specialized shoes.

What footwear is allowed on the turf? +

For football turfs, we recommend turf shoes (TF) or artificial grass boots (AG). Metal studs are strictly prohibited as they damage the surface.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking? +

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your slot time for a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours are non-refundable.

Are the floodlights included in the price? +

Yes, the cost of LED floodlights is fully included in all evening and night booking slots. There are no hidden charges.
